Voltage Line Upgrade Candidate Generation

VoltageLineUpgradeCandidateGeneration creates lower-impedance conductor upgrade candidates for transformer measurement zones with valuable voltage problems.

Finding problematic transformer zones​

The generator reads transformer measurement-zone rows from network_performance_metrics_enhanced, joined to link_to_input for the input year. Rows are limited to the requested work package, scenario, feeders, and year range.

A row qualifies when it has maximum and minimum section-voltage results, positive estimated value, and breaches either the configured voltage-spread or average-delta threshold:

voltageSwingPu = maximumSectionVoltage - minimumSectionVoltage

improvementFactor = min(
  targetVoltageSwingPu / voltageSwingPu,       when swing is breached
  voltageDeltaThresholdPu / voltageDeltaMax    when delta is breached
) / targetHeadroomFactor

The current value estimate is the sum of load VCR and generation CECV associated with the voltage issue. Rows for the same feeder, transformer, and year are combined by summing value and retaining the lowest improvement factor. Currently, voltage swing includes the effects of tap settings and head voltage, so the resulting upgrade may overstate the amount of voltage correction needed in the lines.

For each transformer zone, sizingLookaheadYears limits the years used to derive the improvement factor to the inclusive window from its first threshold breach through firstBreachYear + sizingLookaheadYears. The default of 0 uses only the first breach year; null uses every threshold-breaking year in the requested range. For example, breaches in 2028, 2030, and 2031 with a lookahead of 2 use 2028 and 2030 to choose the strictest improvement factor. All three years still contribute voltage summaries and dollar value.

Finding related lines and their per-length voltage deltas​

For each qualifying transformer, the generator finds upstream lines by tracing toward the feeder head and downstream lines in the transformer's LV feeder container. Their original CIM mRIDs are mapped through cim_to_opendss to simplified/OpenDSS lines. A line is categorised as upstream when one of its mapped original lines is in the upstream trace; otherwise it is categorised as internal to the transformer measurement zone.

For each simplified line and year, individual phase rows from asset_level_voltage_summary are combined using the largest average absolute voltage delta and the largest individual phase voltage spread (max_delta - min_delta). Rows with phase = 'ALL' are excluded because they already aggregate phases. The line's average absolute voltage delta per metre is:

lineLengthM = maximumDownstreamDistanceM - minimumUpstreamDistanceM
lineDeltaPuPerM = avgAbsDeltaPu / lineLengthM

This means candidate generation uses the worst reported phase delta p.u. per metre for a line. Across the requested year range, a line is considered for an upgrade when it exceeds its applicable target in at least one contributing year.

Calculating separate upstream and internal targets​

Each transformer-zone/year has an upstream target and an internal target. A category has no target when the zone has no summary lines in that category. Otherwise:

targetUpstreamDeltaPuPerM = maximum upstream line delta p.u. per metre * improvementFactor
targetInternalDeltaPuPerM = maximum internal line delta p.u. per metre * improvementFactor

The two categories are calculated independently, so a weak internal line cannot set the upstream target and vice versa. A line is eligible only when it has a positive length, meets minAvgAbsDeltaPu, and its observed delta p.u. per metre is greater than its category target. Lines already at or below target are not upgraded.

Estimating captured value​

Every threshold-breaking year contributes its full measurement-zone risk value, regardless of sizingLookaheadYears. The required swing improvement for a zone/year is:

targetSwingImprovementPu = voltageSwingPu * (1 - improvementFactor)

For an individual line, its swing contribution is the largest individual phase spread, floored at zero. The benefit of replacing its conductor is estimated from the geometric change in the positive-sequence impedance vector:

lineSwingContributionPu = max(maxAcrossPhases(maxDeltaPu - minDeltaPu), 0)

impedanceReductionFraction =
  magnitude(originalImpedanceVector - upgradedImpedanceVector)
  / magnitude(originalImpedanceVector)

swingBenefitPu = lineSwingContributionPu * impedanceReductionFraction

capturedFraction = clamp(
  swingBenefitPu * effectivenessFactor / targetSwingImprovementPu,
  0,
  1
)

capturedValue = yearValue * capturedFraction

For a simplified line representing multiple original lines, the smallest geometric impedance reduction is used. The upstream or internal effectiveness factor changes captured value, while eligibility and conductor sizing continue to use the delta p.u. targets alone.

Selecting a conductor​

Per-length voltage delta is assumed to scale with line impedance. For every zone/year contribution to a candidate:

requiredImpedanceRatio = targetDeltaPuPerM / observedDeltaPuPerM

The strictest, lowest ratio across the relevant years and zones controls conductor selection. The headroom divisor is already included in the target delta p.u. per metre and is not applied again.

A catalogue conductor must be compatible with every mapped original line by voltage, phase, wire class, and rated current. The selected conductor must satisfy the required impedance ratio for every mapped original line. Among suitable conductors, the cheapest is selected, with impedance, rated current, and ID as deterministic tie breakers.

If none meets the target, the generator selects the compatible conductor with the lowest impedance, provided it strictly reduces impedance for every mapped original line. Candidates are also rejected for missing mappings or metadata, short or zero-length lines, and non-positive captured value.

Ranking and limiting candidates​

Candidates are ranked by captured value per metre of mapped original line. In that order, the generator accumulates captured value for each measurement zone across all of its years. A candidate is dropped when every measurement zone to which it contributes has already accumulated at least its total all-year value. This models the higher-ranked candidates as having already corrected all valued swing problems in those zones while retaining a candidate that can still benefit any uncovered zone.

Candidate info identifies the line as UPSTREAM or INTERNAL in line_category and reports original and selected impedance per metre. The breakdown has a separate entry for every contributing measurement-zone/year, which is uniquely identified by transformer_mrid, feeder, and year. Each entry includes:

  • transformer_mrid, feeder, and year;
  • zone_metrics, holding values that are invariant for the measurement-zone/year and so are identical across every candidate line it contributes to:
    • total_value (the zone's value summed across all years) and year_value;
    • voltage_swing_pu and improvement_factor;
    • target_upstream_delta_pu_per_m when an upstream target exists; and
    • target_internal_delta_pu_per_m when an internal target exists;
  • the line-specific captured_value, line_swing_contribution_pu, impedance_reduction_fraction, effectiveness_factor, and avg_abs_delta_pu_per_m.

The target swing improvement, swing benefit, and captured fraction are omitted because they can be calculated from the stored inputs above; captured_fraction can also be recovered as captured_value / zone_metrics.year_value.

Outcome logging​

Detailed decisions remain at debug level. Customer-visible summary messages report counts for missing mappings, missing metadata, short or zero-length lines, incompatible conductors, conductors that cannot reduce impedance, fallback selections, and zero captured value.
